## 3. Social Impact

The company invested 2.1 million USD in community programs across four regions
in 2025. Programs covered STEM education partnerships, vocational training for
underrepresented groups, and local water-stewardship projects. Employee
volunteering reached 9,400 hours, and the company maintained its commitment to
pay equity across all regions where it operates. Community program governance
is reviewed annually by the board's sustainability committee, and results are
published in the annual impact statement. The long-term goal is to scale the
most effective programs while phasing out initiatives that fail to meet their
measured impact targets, reallocating funds toward partnerships with
demonstrated outcomes in education access, workforce development, and
ecosystem restoration. In 2026 the company plans to expand the vocational
training program to two additional regions and to pilot a new community grant
scheme administered with local nonprofit partners, with progress reported
quarterly through internal dashboards and annually in this report series.
Beyond the headline programs, the company also funds a regional smallholder
agriculture initiative that reached 1,800 farmers with irrigation and soil
training last year, and a digital-literacy campaign that certified 2,300
participants across rural community centers. Both initiatives are evaluated
through independent third-party measurement partners using pre-registered
outcomes frameworks, and results feed directly into the next funding cycle
decisions.
